# Acknowledgements / 致谢

## English

**WRobot - sdevil Enhanced** exists because of the original work shared by the
wheeled-legged robot community.

Special thanks to:

- **Mu Shibo (穆世博)** for creating and publishing the original
  [Micro-Wheeled_leg-Robot](https://github.com/MuShibo/Micro-Wheeled_leg-Robot),
  including its mechanical design, electronics, ESP32 control architecture, and
  balancing implementation.
- **Li Yufeng (李育锋)**, listed by the original project as an open-source
  contributor.
- The authors and maintainers of SimpleFOC, FastLED, NimBLE-Arduino, SCServo,
  MaixPy, and the other libraries used by this project.

The current project is organized and maintained by **sdevil**, with the goal of
extending the platform toward AI vision, voice interaction, external agents, and
richer wheeled-legged behaviors while preserving the ESP32 as the real-time
safety and motion controller.
